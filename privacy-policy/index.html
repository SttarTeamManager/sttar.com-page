Legal bases (EEA/UK):

(i) Consent (for marketing emails)

(ii) Legitimate interests (site security/operations)

(iii) Legal obligation (where required)

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

We retain your contact details while you remain on the waitlist or until you unsubscribe.

Your information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate.
